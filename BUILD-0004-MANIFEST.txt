REMOTE BODY FOUNDATION
- Player consciousness is decoupled from one permanent chassis.
- Destroyed mech remains as a wreck at the death location.
- Spare assembled bodies tracked.
- Death consumes a spare body when available.
- No spare chassis => temporary mini recovery bot mode.

BODY DAMAGE / SALVAGE
- Core, head, left/right arm, left/right leg, weapon have separate HP.
- Enemy attacks select a body-part hitbox.
- Wreck stores per-part remaining HP.
- Salvage percentage is derived from surviving component condition.
- High local damage reduces salvage disproportionately.

LASER
- Visible core beam restored.
- Additional low-cost glow lines and muzzle pulse.
- Material-specific obstacle penetration resistance.
- Light debris can be penetrated.
- Dense structures reduce penetration/energy.
- Hard barrier stops beam.
- Laser damage uses the same penetration trace as the visual.

WORLD FOUNDATION
- Existing arena obstacle collision retained.
- Base land/orbit distinction and real fabrication/salvage economy intentionally left for the persistent-world phase.
