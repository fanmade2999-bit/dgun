SUMMON SYSTEM
- 32 runtime summon slots.
- NPCs have distinct visual variants and local wandering behavior.
- Bosses are larger moving actors that pursue the player.
- Summoned NPCs/bosses are independent from the legacy enemy/boss slots.
- /list reports active counts.
- Player laser can damage summoned actors when COMBAT is enabled.
- Summoned actors are currently session-scoped and are not saved.
